Hardcore Nuzlocke rules

The classic Nuzlocke plus the Hardcore rules. The tracker checks them as you play.

  • You may only catch the first wild Pokémon you meet in each area (route, town, cave; a multi-floor place counts once). If it faints or flees, that area is spent.
  • Dupes clause: a Pokémon whose evolution line you already have doesn't count; the next new one does. Encounters before you have Poké Balls don't count either.
  • Nickname every Pokémon you get, the starter included. Its species name (e.g. PIDGEY for a Pidgey) isn't a nickname.
  • A Pokémon that faints is dead and may never battle again: sending it out, as the lead or by a switch, breaks the rule. It may stay in the party, and a Pokémon Center heals it like the others.
  • A wipe (every living Pokémon fainted; the dead don't count, healed or not) ends the run. So does having no Pokémon left that may battle: every living one, in the party and the PC, over the level cap.
  • No items in battle (Poké Balls excepted). Healing between battles is fine.
  • Level caps: a Pokémon over the highest level of the next Gym Leader may stay in the party but must not battle — it may not lead or be sent out. Passing the cap during a battle is fine; from the next battle on it sits out until the cap goes up. In the League the cap is the last Elite Four member's highest level, and it may be passed once the League's battles have begun. Outside battle, the line about your lead in each result ends with the current cap ("cap Lv14").
  • Battle style must be Set.
  • Gifts (and the starter) don't use up an area's encounter. Win by beating the Champion.
  • Hard level caps (option): a Pokémon at the level cap gains no more levels — experience and Rare Candies stop there.
  • First rival battle (option): it doesn't count — a Pokémon that faints in it isn't dead, and losing it doesn't end the run.
